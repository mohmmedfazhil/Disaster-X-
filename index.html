      Emergency Protocols

      Rescue mate AI Hub

      ROAD ACCIDENT / TRAUMA

      Severe crash first aid

      Call emergency services. Protect yourself from traffic and fire. Do not move someone with possible head, neck, or back injury unless the scene is unsafe or CPR/bleeding control is needed. Monitor breathing and control severe bleeding.

      CARDIAC / CPR

      Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation

      Compress chest center 100-120 BPM, 2 inches deep. Full recoil. Deploy AED.

      HEMORRHAGE

      Severe Bleeding & Tourniquets

      Continuous direct pressure. Apply commercial tourniquet 2-3 inches above wound.

      STRUCTURAL / FIRE

      Evacuation & Smoke Survival

      Crawl 12-24 inches from floor. Check doors before opening. Never take elevators.

      For life-threatening injuries, call your local emergency number now. These steps are general first aid guidance, not a substitute for emergency dispatch or trained responders.
